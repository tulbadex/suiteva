# Suiteva — Image Generation Prompts

Use these prompts in any AI image generator (DALL-E, Midjourney, Canva AI, Leonardo AI, etc.)

## Brand Colors Reference
- Deep Ocean Blue: `#0F4C75`
- Amber Gold: `#E8A838`
- Ice White: `#F7F9FC`
- Midnight: `#1A1A2E`

---

## 1. Logo

> **Prompt:** Create a modern, clean logo for "Suiteva" — a hotel reservation and management platform. The logo should feature the text "Suiteva" in a sleek sans-serif font where the double "n" is subtly stylized. Use deep ocean blue (#0F4C75) as the primary color with an amber gold (#E8A838) accent on the "Inn" portion or as an underline. Include a minimal icon element — either a stylized S that morphs into a hotel suite door or bed silhouette. Style: modern, tech-forward, professional. Transparent background, PNG format, 300x80px.

---

## 2. Favicon

> **Prompt:** Create a minimal square favicon icon for "Suiteva" hotel reservation app. A stylized letter "B" combined with a small door or key symbol. Deep ocean blue (#0F4C75) with an amber gold (#E8A838) accent detail. Simple, recognizable at 32x32px and 16x16px sizes. Square format, PNG with transparent background, 512x512px output.

After generating, convert at https://favicon.io/favicon-converter/ to get:
- `favicon.ico`
- `favicon-96x96.png`
- `favicon.svg`
- `apple-touch-icon.png`
- `site.webmanifest`
- `web-app-manifest-192x192.png`
- `web-app-manifest-512x512.png`
